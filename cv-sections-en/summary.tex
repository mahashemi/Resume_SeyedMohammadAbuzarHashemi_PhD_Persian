\cvsection{Professional Profile}

Seyed Mohammad Abuzar Hashemi is an AI researcher and Applied Scientist with a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering, working at the intersection of building AI at industrial scale and training the people who will build its next generation. That instinct for connecting theory to practice first took shape at the University at Buffalo, where he taught machine learning and deep learning while conducting applied AI research with industry partners during his doctoral studies.

At Amazon, he has led the development of multimodal, agentic AI assistants that turn advances in large language models into real-world, task-oriented products across domains, developing approaches that can be adapted to new applications and industries. This work includes Amazon One Medical's Health AI and its 2026 expansion to Amazon.com and the Amazon app, with research spanning multimodal reasoning, safety, reliability, clinical evaluation, and production-scale AI.
