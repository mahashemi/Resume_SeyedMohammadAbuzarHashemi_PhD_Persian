\cvsection{Professional Profile}

Seyed Mohammad Abuzar Hashemi is an Artificial Intelligence researcher and Applied Scientist with a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering and over a decade of experience spanning AI research, production systems, graduate teaching, and curriculum design. His work lives at the intersection of building agentic AI at industrial scale and teaching the people who will build its next generation.

At Amazon Health AI, he has been a key scientific contributor to the development of agentic healthcare AI, including the 2026 Health AI expansion to Amazon customers and Amazon One Medical's Health AI. His research and evaluation of patient-facing agents centers on safety, reliability, clinical context, multi-turn evaluation, and multimodal reasoning -- consistently carrying research methods through to production systems.

That same instinct for connecting theory to practice shaped his years at the University at Buffalo, where he taught Introduction to Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Advanced Machine Learning to classes of 50--100+ students, coordinated over 50 Master's project groups, and designed applied deep learning workshops -- and it continues to guide how he approaches AI education today, connecting strong foundations with empirical research, real-world projects, rigorous evaluation, safety, and ethics.
