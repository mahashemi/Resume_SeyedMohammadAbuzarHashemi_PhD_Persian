\cvsection{Professional Profile}

Seyed Mohammad Abuzar Hashemi is an AI researcher and Applied Scientist with a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering, combining production-scale agentic AI development with graduate teaching and curriculum design -- work that sits at the intersection of building AI at industrial scale and training the people who will build its next generation.

At Amazon, he has led the development of multimodal, agentic AI assistants that translate large language model capabilities into real-world, task-oriented products across healthcare and commerce -- including Amazon One Medical's Health AI and its 2026 expansion to Amazon.com and the Amazon app, with research centered on safety, reliability, and clinical evaluation. At the University at Buffalo, that same instinct for connecting theory to practice shaped years of teaching machine learning and deep learning to 100+ student classes, coordinating 50+ Master's project groups, and designing applied curricula -- an approach that continues to guide how he thinks about AI education today.
