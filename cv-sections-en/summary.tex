\cvsection{Summary}

Seyed Mohammad Abuzar Hashemi is an Artificial Intelligence researcher and Applied Scientist with a Ph.D. in Computer Science, specializing in multimodal learning, computer vision, NLP, large language models, and agentic AI. His work spans AI research, production-scale systems, healthcare AI, evaluation, and the translation of research into deployable products.

At Amazon Health AI, he has been a key scientific contributor to agentic healthcare AI, including the 2026 expansion of Health AI to Amazon customers and the launch of Amazon One Medical's Health AI. His research includes PatientAgentBench and IMCBench, with a focus on rigorous evaluation, safety, clinical context, multimodal reasoning, and patient-facing AI agents.

He also brings academic teaching, curriculum design, graduate mentoring, technical leadership, and research-community leadership experience, with a strong interest in building AI research and education programs.
