\cvsection{Summary}

Seyed Mohammad Abuzar Hashemi is an Artificial Intelligence researcher, Applied Scientist, and academic leader with a Ph.D. in Computer Science, bringing together production-scale agentic AI development, peer-reviewed research, and hands-on graduate teaching and curriculum design. This combination -- deploying agentic AI at industry scale while building the courses and programs that train the next generation of AI practitioners -- positions him to lead an academic AI department: setting research direction, modernizing curricula around LLMs and agentic systems, and connecting faculty and students to industry practice.

At Amazon Health AI, he has been a key scientific contributor to agentic healthcare AI, including the 2026 expansion of Health AI to Amazon customers and the launch of Amazon One Medical's Health AI, leading research and evaluation of patient-facing AI agents with a focus on rigorous evaluation, safety, clinical context, and multimodal reasoning -- work that translates directly into applied, industry-grounded coursework and research supervision.

As a graduate teaching assistant at the University at Buffalo, he taught and evaluated core AI and machine learning courses -- including Introduction to Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Advanced Machine Learning -- to classes of 50-100+ students, coordinated dozens of Master's project teams, and later designed and led applied deep learning workshops as President of the Computer Science and Engineering Graduate Student Association. He combines this direct classroom and curriculum-design experience with technical leadership, mentoring, and research-community leadership, and a strong interest in building and leading AI research and education programs at the departmental level.
