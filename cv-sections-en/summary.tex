\cvsection{Professional Profile}

At Amazon Health AI, he has been a key scientific contributor to agentic healthcare AI, including the 2026 Health AI expansion to Amazon customers and Amazon One Medical's Health AI, with research centered on safety, reliability, and clinical evaluation. At the University at Buffalo, that same instinct for connecting theory to practice shaped years of teaching machine learning and deep learning to 100+ student classes, coordinating 50+ Master's project groups, and designing applied curricula -- an approach that continues to guide how he thinks about AI education today.
